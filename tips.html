Local Tips

Small practical details that make Puerto Vallarta easier to enjoy.

Airport Arrival

Uber pickup at the airport can be confusing. Follow app guidance carefully and allow extra time during busy arrivals.

Money & ATMs

Use bank-affiliated ATMs when possible and carry some pesos for small purchases.

Tipping

Restaurants commonly expect 10–20% depending on service. Cash tips are appreciated.

Drinking Water

Use the filtered faucet setting for drinking water in the condo.

Sun & Heat

The sun is strong, even on cloudy days. Use sunscreen, hydrate often, and take breaks in the shade.

Beach Safety

Check beach flags before swimming and be mindful of waves and currents.

Uber vs Taxi

Uber is usually easiest for guests. Taxis are common but confirm pricing before riding.

Markets

Local markets are great for souvenirs, snacks, produce, and casual browsing.
