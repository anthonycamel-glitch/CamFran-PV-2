Building Information

Everything guests need to know about the condo, rooftop, elevator, water, and departure.

Elevator Access

  1. The unit is on the second floor.
  2. Upon arrival, use the stairs first.
  3. Enter the condo using your access instructions.
  4. Unlock the elevator door from inside.
  5. The elevator is then available during your stay and for departure luggage.

Filtered Drinking Water

  1. The kitchen sink has a 3-stage filter.
  2. Turn the faucet handle fully right.
  3. Let water run for a few seconds.
  4. Use for drinking, coffee, cooking, and reusable bottles.

Shared Rooftop Pool

  • Shared by only four residences
  • Open daily: 8:00 AM – 10:00 PM
  • No glass in pool area
  • Please respect neighbors and quiet hours

Before You Leave

  • Turn off the A/C
  • Lock all doors and windows
  • Leave used towels in the bathroom
  • Place trash in designated bins
  • Return keys according to checkout instructions
